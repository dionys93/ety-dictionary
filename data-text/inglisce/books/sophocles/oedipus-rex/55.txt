MESSENGER

. . . [if] þat’s þe ríson you’re [a] [fugitive]
and uilln’t goe hoame.


OEDIPUS

I fiared Apollo’s prophecie
máit reviele itselfe in mi.


MESSENGER

You uere afréid
you máit become corropted þru your pârents?


OEDIPUS

Ћat’s roiht, oald man. Ћat uas my [constant] fiare.


MESSENGER

Are you aüair þise fiars [of] yours are [groundless]?


OEDIPUS

And uoie is þat? [If] I uas beired þeir c̃oild . . .


MESSENGER

Becase you and Polybus uere not relâted.


OEDIPUS

Uate dou you mine? Uas not Polybus my faþre?


MESSENGER

Hie uas as moc̃ your faþre as þis man hiere,
no [more], no [less].


OEDIPUS

But hôe cane eny man
hu mines [nothing] to mi bie jost þe sâm
as my oan faþre?


MESSENGER

But Polybus uas not your faþre, no [more] þan I am.
