OEDIPUS

Uate is it [then]
you’re asching mi to dou?


CHORUS LEADER

Paie Creon dû respecte.
Hie has not bign [foolish] in þe [past], and nôe
þat oaþ hie’s sueired has pôure.


OEDIPUS

Are you aüair
jost uate you’re asching?


CHORUS LEADER

[Yes]. I onderstande.


OEDIPUS

[Then] telle mi clierly uate you mine to saie.
