JOCASTA

You leading citasans [of] Thebes, I þinc
it is apprópriate for mi to visit
aur [gods][’] sâcrid [shrines], beiring in my [hands]
þis [garland] and anoþre [offering] [of] incense.
For Oedipus has leted excessif peine
seise on his harte and dus not onderstande
uate’s hapaning nôe by þincing [of] þe [past],
lîc [a] man uiþ sense. [Instead] hie lissanes to
[whoever] speics to him [of] [dreadful] þings.
I cane dou [nothing] [more] uiþ my advîse,
and [so], Lyceian Apollo, I come to you,
hu stande hiere besîde os, [a] sopliant,
uiþ [offerings] and prayers for you to fînde
some uaie [of] clainsing uate corrops os.
For nôe uie are afréid, jost lîc þose
hu on [a] scipe sihe þeir [helmsman] terrifaed.

[JOCASTA setes her [offerings] on þe altre. [A] [MESSENGER] entres, anoþre oald man.]


[MESSENGER]

Strângers, cane you telle mi ouere I fînde
þe hause [of] Oedipus, your cyngue? Ouel [yet],
[if] you gnoe, cane you telle mi ouere hie is?


CHORUS LEADER

His hoame is hiere, strânger, and hie’s insîde.
Ћis [lady] is þe maþre [of] his c̃ildren.


MESSENGER

Maie her happy hoame aluays bie [blessed],
for sie is his cuine, treu [mistress] [of] his hause.


JOCASTA

I uisce þe sâm for you, strânger. Your fîn uords
maic you desêve as moc̃. But telle os nôe
uoie you have comed. Dou you seic informâcion,
