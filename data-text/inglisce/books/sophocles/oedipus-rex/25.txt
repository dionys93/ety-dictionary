CHORUS

Speicing from þe Delphic roc
þe [oracular] voice [intoned] [a] nâme.
But hu is þe man, þe uan
hu uiþ his blode-red [hands]
has doud [unspeakable] brutaletie?
Ћe tîme has comed for him to flihe—
to mouve his [powerful] fûte
[more] [swiftly] þan þose hôfs
[of] horses [riding] lîc [a] storme.
Against him Zeus’s sone nôe springs,
armed uiþ loihtning fŷre and leading on
þe [inexorable] and [terrifying] Furies.

From þe [snowy] pícs [of] [mount] Parnassus
þe [message] has jost flasced, ordiring all
to seic þe uan [whom] no uan gnoes.
Lîc [a] uîld bûlle hie uandres nôe,
hîded in þe [untamed] uôde,
þru rox and câves, alone
uiþ his despeire on [joyless] fyte,
chieping his distance from þat doumed
[uttered] at Erþ’s central nâvele stone.
But þat fâtal [oracle] [still] livfes,
hoviring above his hede [forever].
