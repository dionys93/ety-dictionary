[if] hie seûd become anoþre [honoured] gaiste
in my oan hoame and uiþ my gnollage,

to maic seure all þise ordirs taic effecte,
for my saic, for þe saic [of] þe [god],
and for aur bairan, [godless], [ruined] [land].
