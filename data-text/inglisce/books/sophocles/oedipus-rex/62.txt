OEDIPUS [lôching aut [away] from þe palace]

You [elders], þó I’ve nevre sihed þe man
uie’ve bign seicing for [a] lòng tîme nôe,
[if] I had to guesse, I þinc I sihe him.
Hie’s coming hiere. Hie lôcs [very] oald—
as is apprópriate, [if] hie’s þe uan.
And I gnoe þe pípuls coming uiþ him,
[servants] [of] mîne. But [if] you’ve sihed him before,
you’ll recognyse him ouel þan I uill.


CHORUS LEADER

[Yes], I recognyse þe man. Ћere’s no dôute.
Hie uirched for Laius—[a] [trusty] [shepherd].

[Enter SERVANT, anoþre oald [shepherd].]


OEDIPUS

Stranger from Corinth, lete mi first asc you—
is þis þe man you spoac [of]?


MESSENGER

[Yes], hie is—
hie’s þe man you sihe in fronte [of] you.


OEDIPUS

You, oald man, over hiere. Lôc at mi.
Nôe ansere uate I asc. Some tîme [ago]
did you uirc for Laius?


SERVANT

[Yes], as [a] slâve.
But I uas not byhed. I groed [up] in his hause.
