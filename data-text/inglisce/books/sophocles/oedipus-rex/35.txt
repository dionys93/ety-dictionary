CREON

to ponișe mi in one [of] two [dreadful] uays—

or arreste mi and [then] have mi chilled.


OEDIPUS

Ћat’s roiht.
[Lady], I caic̃ed him comitting tríson,
[a] viceus crîme against mi personally.


CREON

Lete mi not prosper but daihe [a] man [accursed],
[if] I have doud uate you accuse mi [of].


JOCASTA

Oedipus,
for þe saic [of] þe [gods], treuste him in þis.
Respecte þat oaþ hie maicd before all hevon—
dou it for my saic and for þose arônd you.


CHORUS LEADER

I beg you, my [lord], consente to þis—
