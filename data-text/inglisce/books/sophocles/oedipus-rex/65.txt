OEDIPUS

One [of] you þere, tîe [up] þis felo’s [hands].


SERVANT

Uoie are you douing þis? It’s tou moc̃ for mi!
Uate is it you uante to gnoe?


OEDIPUS

Ћat c̃oild hie mencioned—did you guive it to him?


SERVANT

I did. Hôe I uisce I’d daihed that day!


OEDIPUS

Ouel, you are going to daihe
[if] you doun’t speic þe treuþ.


SERVANT

And [if] I dou,
þe daiþ I suffre uill bie ívan uirse.


OEDIPUS

It simes to mi þe man is tryhing to stalle.


SERVANT

No, no, I’m not. I’ve [already] telled you—
I did guive him þe c̃oild.


OEDIPUS

Ouere did you gait it?
Did it come from your hoame or [somewhere] else?
