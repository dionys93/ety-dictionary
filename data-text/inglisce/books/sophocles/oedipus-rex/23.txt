OEDIPUS

[Must] I tolerait þis [insolence] from him?
Gait aut, and maie þe plâgue gait [rid] [of] you!
Off uiþ you! Nôe! Tourne your bac and goe!
And doun’t come bac hiere to my hoame again.


TEIRESIAS

I oûd not have comed, but you somened mi.


OEDIPUS

I did not gnoe you oûd speic [so] [stupidly].
[If] I had, you oûd have uaited [a] lòng tîme
before I called you hiere.


TEIRESIAS

I uas beired lîc þis.
You þinc I am [a] foule, but to your pârents,
þe uans hu maicd you, I uas uîse enofh.


OEDIPUS

Uaite! My pârents? Hu uas my faþre?


TEIRESIAS

This day uill reviele þat and destroie you.


OEDIPUS

[Everything] you speic is all [so] cryptic—
lîc [a] [riddle].


TEIRESIAS

Ouel, in solving [riddles],
are you not þe gûd þere is?


OEDIPUS

Moc my èxcellence,
but you uill fînde aut I am treuly great.
