OEDIPUS

[Alas]! Indide, [lady], uoie seûd eny man
paie dû [reverence] to Apollo’s [shrine],
ouere his prophet livfes, or to þose birds
uic̃ scrime aut [overhead]? For þey [foretold]
þat I uas going to mourder my oan faþre.
But nôe hie’s daid and lîes benieþ þe Erþ,
and I am hiere. I nevre touc̃ed my spiere.
[Perhaps] hie daihed from [a] desîre to sihe mi—
[so] in þat sense I bringued abaut his daiþ.
But as for þose prophetic [oracles],
þey’re [worthless]. Polybus has taicd þem
to [Hades], ouere hie lîes.


JOCASTA

Uas I not þe uan
hu [predicted] þis some tîme [ago]?


OEDIPUS

You did,
but [then] I uas [misguided] by my fiars.


JOCASTA

You [must] not chiepe on fylling [up] your harte
uiþ all þise þings.


OEDIPUS

But my maþre’s bede—
[Surely] I seûd [still] bie afréid [of] þat?
