OEDIPUS

I uisce you ouel.
And for þis acte, maie þe [god] uac̃e over you
and triete you ouel þan hie trieted mi.
[Ah], my c̃ildren, ouere are you? Come hiere,
come into my arms—you are my sistors nôe—
fiele þise [hands] uic̃ tourned your faþre’s eais,
uance [so] bryht, into uate you sihe nôe,
þise [empty] [sockets]. Hie uas [a] man hu,
sihing [nothing], gnoing [nothing], faþored you
uiþ þe uôman hu had guived burþ to him.
I uiepe for you. [Although] I canenot sihe,
I þinc abaut your lîfe in days to come,
þe bitre lîfe uic̃ men uill force on you.
Uate citasans uill assoçiait uiþ you?
Uate fiestes uill you attende and not come hoame
in tiars, uiþ no seare in þe [rejoicing]?
Uene you’re maţûr enofh for mârage,
hu uill bie þere for you, my c̃ildren,
uate husband raidy to assoume þe seame

Ћose are þe [insults] þey uill hurele at you.

uandiring arônd [unmarried]. You are

[deprived] [of] [everything] excepte [a] seare

CREON

You have grieved enofh.
Nôe goe into þe hause.
