OEDIPUS

Speic aut to [everyone]. Ћe griefe I fiele
for þise citasans is ívan great
þan eny peine I fiele for my oan lîfe.


CREON

[Then] lete mi reporte uate I hyred from þe [god].
[Lord] Phoebus clierly ordres os to drîve [away]
þe [polluting] staine þis [land] has [harboured].
It uill not bie hialed [if] uie chiepe noursing it.


OEDIPUS

Uate sorte [of] [cleansing]? And þis desastre—
hôe did it hapane?


CREON

By banișement—
or [atone] for mourdre by sceding blode again,
for blode brings on þe storme uic̃ [blasts] aur stâte.


OEDIPUS

And þe uan huse fâte þe [god] revieled—
uate sorte [of] man is hie?


CREON

Before you comed, my [lord],
to stiere aur scipe [of] stâte, Laius reuled þis [land].


OEDIPUS

I have hyred þat, but I nevre sahe þe man.


CREON

Laius uas chilled. And nôe þe [god] is clier:
þose mourdrors, hie telles os, [must] bie ponișed,
[whoever] þey maie bie.
