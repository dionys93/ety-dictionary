OEDIPUS

And ouere are þey?
In uate contrie? Ouere am I to fînde [a] traisse
[of] þis ainc̃ent crîme? It uill bie hard to trac.


CREON

Hiere in Thebes, [so] saied þe [god]. Uate is seicd
is fônde, but uate is [overlooked] escâpes.


OEDIPUS

Uene Laius falled in blody daiþ, ouere uas hie—
at hoame, or in his fields, or in [another] [land]?


CREON

Hie uas [abroad], on his uaie to Delphi—
þat’s uate hie telled os. Hie biguind þe [trip],
but did not retourne.


OEDIPUS

Uas þere no [messenger]—
no compañon hu maicd þe journie uiþ him
and uitnaced uate taicd plâce—[a] person
hu máit provîde some gnollage men coûd use?


CREON

Ћey all daihed—excepte for uan hu uas afréid
and roned [away]. Ћere uas only one þingue
hie coûd informe os [of] uiþ [confidence]
abaut þe þings hie sahe.
