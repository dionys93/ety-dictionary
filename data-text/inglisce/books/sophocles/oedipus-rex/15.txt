OEDIPUS

Uate cŷnd [of] [rumours]?
I’m lôching into evry [story].


CHORUS LEADER

It uas saied þat Laius uas chilled by certan [travellers].


OEDIPUS

[Yes], I hyred as moc̃. 
But no uan has sihed þe uan hu did it.


CHORUS LEADER

Ouel, [if] þe [killer] has eny fiars, 
uance hie hyres your curses on him,
hie uill not hoalde bac, for þey are sírieus.


OEDIPUS

Uene [a] man has no fiare [of] douing þe acte,
hie’s not afréid [of] uords.


CHORUS LEADER

No, not in þe caisse
ouere no uan standes þere to [convict] him.
But at [last] Teiresias is bying guîded hiere,
aur [god]-lîc prophet, in [whom] treuþ [resides]
[more] [so] þan in all oþre men.


[Enter TEIRESIAS lead by [a] small BOIE.]
