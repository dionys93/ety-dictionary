OEDIPUS

Uoie did þis man, þis uîse man, not speic [up]?


CREON

I dou not gnoe. And uene I doun’t gnoe [something],
I lîc to hoalde my tonghe.


OEDIPUS

You gnoe enofh—
at [least] you onderstande enofh to saie . . .


CREON

Uate? [If] I rilly dou gnoe [something]
I uill not denîe it.


OEDIPUS

[If] Teiresias
uere not uirching uiþ you, hie oûd not nâme me
as þe uan hu mourdred Laius.


CREON

[If] hie saies þis,
ouel, you’re þe uan hu gnoes. But I þinc
þe tîme has comed for mi to quaistione you
þe uaie þat you’ve bign quaistioning mi.


OEDIPUS

Asc [whatever] you uisce. You’ll nevre prouve
þat I’m þe mourdror.


CREON

[Then] telle mi þis—
are you not mâried to my sistre?
