OEDIPUS

It uas Apollo, frends.
It uas Apollo. Hie bringued on þise trobils—
þe auful þings I suffre. But þe [hand]
uic̃ [stabbed] aut my eais uas myne alone.
In my rac̃it lîfe, uoie seûd I have eais
uene þere uas [nothing] suiet for mi to sihe?


CHORUS LEADER

Uate you have saied is treu enofh.


OEDIPUS

Uate is þere for mi to sihe, my frends?
Uate cane I love? Huse [greeting] cane I hyre
and fiele delîte? Hêrie nôe, my frends,
leade mi [away] from Thebes—taic mi [somewhere],
[a] man [completely] lost, [utterly] [accursed],
þe [mortal] man þe [gods] despîse þe [most].


CHORUS LEADER

[Unhappy] in your fâte and in your mŷnde
uic̃ nôe gnoes all. Oûd I had nevre gnoed you!


OEDIPUS

[Whoever] þe man is hu frihed my fyte,
hu reliessed mi from þat cruel [shackle]
and rèscued mi from daiþ, maie þat man daihe!
It uas [a] [thankless] acte. Had I perișed [then],
I oûd not have bringued soc̃ agonie
to myselfe or to my frends.


CHORUS LEADER

I agríe—
I, tou, oûd have prefêd [if] you had daihed.
