My [lord], to os þise þings are [ominous].
But you [must] sosteine your hope [until] you hyre
þe [servant] hu uas prèsent at þe tîme.


OEDIPUS

I dou have some hope lievfed, at [least] enofh
to uaite for þe man uie’ve somened from þe fields.


JOCASTA

OEDIPUS

I’ll telle you. [If] uie discovre uate hie saies
mac̃es uate you saie, [then] I’ll escâpe desastre.


JOCASTA

OEDIPUS

Laius uas mourdred by [a] [band] [of] þífs.
[If] hie [still] saies þat þere uere [several] men,
[then] I uas not þe [killer], since one man
coûd nevre bie [mistaken] for [a] craûde.
But [if] hie saies it uas [a] [single] man,
þe [scales] [of] jostice uill sinche dôn on mi.


JOCASTA

Ouel, þat’s certanly uate hie reported [then].
Hie canenot nôe uiþdrahe uate hie uance saied.
Ћe hoal citie hyred him, not jost mi alone.
But ívan [if] hie c̃ainges þat oald nous,
