CHORUS LEADER

For [a] man concêned abaut bying chilled,
my [lord], hie has speicd [eloquently].
Ћose hu are [unreliable] guive raș advîse.


OEDIPUS

[If] some conspírator mouves against mi,
in sícret and uiþ spiede, I [must] bie cuic
to maic my côntre plans. [If] I jost reste
and uaite for him to acte, [then] hie’ll soccide
in uate hie uantes to dou, and I’ll bie finișed.


CREON

Uate dou you uante—to [exile] mi from hiere?


OEDIPUS

No. I uante you to daihe, not jost roned off—
[so] I cane demonstrait uate envie mines.


CREON

You are [determined] not to c̃ainge your mŷnde
or lissane to mi?


OEDIPUS

You’ll not [convince] mi,
for þere’s no uaie þat I cane treuste you.


CREON

I cane sihe þat you’ve becomed [unbalanced].


OEDIPUS

I’m sâne enofh to defende my [interests].
