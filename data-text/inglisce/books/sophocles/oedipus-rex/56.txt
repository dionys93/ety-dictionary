OEDIPUS

[Then] uoie did hie calle mi his sone?


MESSENGER

[If] you [must] gnoe,
hie received you as [a] guifte many years ago.
I guived you to him.


OEDIPUS 

Hie rilly loved mi.
Hôe coûd hie [if] I comed from [someone] else?


MESSENGER

Becase before you comed, hie had no c̃ildren—
þat maicd him love you.


OEDIPUS

Uene you guived mi to him,
had you byhed mi or discovred mi by [chance]?


MESSENGER

I fônde you in Cithaeron’s foreste valis.


OEDIPUS

Uate uere you douing uandiring [up] þere?


MESSENGER

I uas lôching after flocs [of] siepe.


OEDIPUS

You uere [a] [shepherd], jost [a] hîred [servant]
roaming hiere and þere?


MESSENGER

[Yes], my sone, I uas.
But at þat tîme I uas þe uan hu sâved you.
