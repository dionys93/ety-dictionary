OEDIPUS

you’ll gait your uisce. Hyre uate I have to saie
and triete your oan desise—[then] you maie hope
to fînde reliefe from your destresse. I speic

[a] strânger to þe crîme. [If] I alone

uiþaut [a] [single] cleue. But as þings stande,

[a] citasan [of] Thebes, I nôe proclâme

[Whoever] among you gnoes þe man it uas
hu mourdred Laius, sone [of] Labdacus,
I ordre him to reviele it all to mi.

to avoide þe dângere [of] þe mâjor [charge]
by speicing aut against himselfe. [If] [so],
hie uill bie sended aut from þis [land] [unhurt]
