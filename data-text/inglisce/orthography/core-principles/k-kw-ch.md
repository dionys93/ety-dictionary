# Velars: state of the system

## /kw/

Conditioned by what follows. No etymology needed.

| Environment | Spelling | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| before *a*, *o* | `qu` | quac, quaic, quote, quarie, equâte, equâtre |
| before a marked *i* (î, í) | `qu` | quît, quîet, requîer, acquîer, bequíþe |
| before plain *e* | `cu` | frícuent, consecuent, sícuence |
| before plain *i*, initial | `cu` | cuic, cuine |
| before plain *i*, medial | `qü` | liqüide, sqüide |

`Qu` before an *i*-letter holds where a diacritic breaks the minim run.
Initial `cu` + *i* has room in the hand; medial does not.

## /kj/

Never marked. `Qu` holds /kw/ before back vowels, so `cu` there can only be /kj/.

| Case | Spelling | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| before a consonant, final, or silent `-e` | `cu` | cúe, cús |
| before a vowel, root-internal | `cu` | evacuait, evacuâcion |
| at a suffix boundary | `cu`, read from the base | rescuer, evacuee |
| stressed, where `u` could read /ʌ/ | `cú` | cús |

Root-internal `cu` + *e* is /kw/ without exception. Every /kj/ case in that
slot is a suffixed form of a base already marked by `ú` or by `-ait`.

## /k/

| Environment | Spelling | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| before *a*, *o*, *u* | `c` | cat, cutte, complicait, caipt |
| before *e*, *i*, *y* | `qu` | bâqued, bâquing, monquie, dònquie, arqui- |
| final | `c`; `-que` | tâc, baic, conque |

## /g/, /gw/

| Sound | Before *a*, *o*, *u* | Before *e*, *i*, *y* | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| /g/ | `g` | `gu` | bagued, baguing |
| /gw/ | `gu` | `gü` | pengüine, distingüișe |

## /gj/

`Gu` has no spare letter to take /gw/ off its hands the way `qu` does for `cu`,
so both readings are already occupied and the mark is needed.
Grounding: Spanish `ñ`, historically *n* + palatal.

| Case | Spelling | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| before a vowel | `g̃u` | arg̃ue, ambig̃ueus |
| before a consonant | `gu` | regular, figure |
| stressed | `gú` | the *ambiguity* type |

The two velars are not symmetric here, for a structural reason.

## /tʃ/

| Spelling | Environment | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `c̃` | default | caic̃e, boic̃e, coic̃ene, ric̃e |
| `ch` | before *î* | chîld, chîde |
| `ț` | a historical *t* | duț |

Keeping the corpus division preserves the vowel + silent `-e` transparency and
leaves the `ei`/`oi` separator rule stated as it already is.
Cost: `c̃` needs its own justification now that Italian `ch` is retired, and
French-routed words like *archer* sit oddly under it.

## Marks

| Mark | Job |
|---|---|
| `ú`, `í`, `ó` | stressed, tense |
| `â` | /eɪ/; typed on the w key |
| `ü` | a glide `u` after `g`, before *e*, *i*, *y* |
| `g̃` | `u` is a vowel after `g`, before another vowel |
| one top mark per word | a bottom mark (`ç`, `ș`, `ț`, `d̦`) may sit alongside |

## Verbs

| Form | Rule | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| /k/-final infinitive | bare `-c` | to baic, to tâc |
| 3rd person present | `-cs` | baics, taics, seics |
| before a vowel ending | `qu` | bâqued, bâquing |

## Pronoun clitics

| Stem ends in | Form | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| a silent `-e` | apostrophe, elided | sihe'm |
| a consonant | hyphen, *h*- or *þ*- dropped | baic-im, tâc-im, taics-im, sihes-im |

`'M` covers both *him* and *them*; both are *h*-pronouns historically, since
`'em` is Middle English *hem*, not a clipping of *them*. The hyphenated form
names one.

## Open

- Stem vowel in the bare form: `baic` or `bâc`.
- `Oi` after `c` now carries three values: /ɪ/ (coic, coite), the /i/
  separator (boic̃e), and /wɪ/ (vencoișe, pengoin).
- `Qü` forms for *vanquish* and *soliloquy* (vencuișe and solilocuie are the
  cramped medial shape).
- Verb *frequent*: adjective stress (frí-) or second-syllable stress.
- Bare `g` before *e*, *i*, *y* — presumably /dʒ/, not yet stated.
- Past of *take* with the `-im` clitic.

## Corpus changes implied

| Change | Affects |
|---|---|
| `ch` for /k/ → `qu` or `c` | chic (kick), to chiepe, blachest, bôching, ch-and-c-tilde.md, the verb documents |
| `-che` infinitives → bare `-c` | the `kn-` table, great-vowel-shift.md, mâche, côche, gnoche, bâche |
| `q` alone for /k/ → `qu` or `c` | joque, parqued |
| `c` for /kw/ before *ui*, *oi* | coic̃e, vencoișe |
